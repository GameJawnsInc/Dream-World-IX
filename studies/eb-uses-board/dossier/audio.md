## 2. Computed Pan And Volume

**Status:** SURVIVED · **Effort:** medium · **Engine:** stock · **Round:** 1

**Pitch.** Sound stops being a fixed cue and becomes a continuous output channel: one `RunSoundCode3` whose pan and volume operands are RPN expressions, so a script can pan a cue to where the thing actually is and fade it by how far away it is.

**Mechanism (as proposed).** `opcodes.encode(op, *args, arg_flags=)` (eb/opcodes.py:49-64) already emits any operand as a pre-encoded expression blob — verified in source. So `encode(0xC8, 53248, sid, 0, PAN_EXPR, VOL_EXPR, arg_flags=0b11000)` is legal bytes today with no new engine surface, and the expression form escapes the 1-byte operand window the same way `SetTextVariable`'s does (trap #8). Pan comes from the behavior compiler's existing safe perception lane: the per-tick player mirror `Global.Int16[mx]/[mz]` (behavior.py `_mx`/`_mz`, copied behind the staged latch — NOT `obj()`/`B_PTR`/`B_DISTANCEA`, which `_FORBIDDEN_COND` at behavior.py:72 refuses under THE PLAYER-REF EVAL LAW). Pan expr = `const(128) mx tx B_MINUS const(32) B_SHIFT_RIGHT B_PLUS` clamped by a two-arm `B_LT`/`B_GT` ladder to 0..255. Volume expr = a Chebyshev-box ladder (behavior.py `_box`, the same primitive `near()` compiles) selecting one of 5 constants — no squares, no cross products, so the 26-bit envelope is never approached. Everything stays inside the existing ticker entry.

**Mechanism (screen correction).** Two amendments before building. (a) CHECK THE NATIVE LANE FIRST — `_optables.py:430-431` carries two opcodes the kit has never emitted: `SetSoundPosition` (0x89, argsizes [2,2,2,1]) and `SetSoundObjectPosition` (0x8A, [1,1], whose arg0 `eventscan.py:557` already classifies as an object UID). That is the engine's own bind-a-cue-to-an-actor path; if it works it gives continuous positional audio with no RPN ladder, no mirror latency and no budget cost. Disassemble stock fields for 0x89/0x8A first (`eb-src` a noisy field like a waterfall/machine room) and only fall back to expression-valued 0xC8 pan/volume if the native ops turn out unused or dead on Steam. (b) SCOPE THE CLAIM TO ONE-SHOTS — 0xC8's pan/volume are sampled when the cue STARTS; there is no set-volume-on-a-playing-voice operand in the 0xC5-0xC8 family, so "fade it by how far away it is" cannot smoothly ride a sustained sample. The honest deliverable is per-firing placement (footsteps, hits, an ambience re-triggered on a tick), plus re-fire quantization; a true continuous fade needs 0x89/0x8A. Also treat "arg3 = pan, arg4 = volume" as ASSERTED-not-proven — it comes from a chest.py comment, not engine source; confirm by a census of the two operands across stock fields (do they vary with on-screen position?) before shipping vocabulary named `pan`.

- DISTANCE = beep RATE. A frame counter with `B_REM` against a divisor derived from native `B_DISTANCEA(exit_obj)`. Never compute `dx*dx + dz*dz`: a computed intermediate is 26-bit signed (`opcodes.EXPR_VALUE_MAX`), and field coords in the ±8000 band overflow a sum of squares into the VariableSource tag bits. Use the engine's own distance op.
- SURFACE = authored XZ zones, not `B_BGIID`. The kit owns the `.bgi` at build time (`scene/bgi.py`), so the builder emits surface-class rectangles and the tick does point-in-rect compares — the proven region substrate. Footfall TIMING comes from `obj(uid=255).f[7]` animFrame (verified vs field 706, `content/region.py:236-241`) or, cheaper, a distance accumulator on the px/pz deltas.

`B_BGIID` / `B_BGIFLOOR` are demoted to an OPTIONAL compaction, gated behind a read of `DoCalcOperationExt.cs:37-43` on the Windows box, because the adjacent `B_BAFRAME` takes a BGI-domain index, not a uid (`memoria-patches/s37-netsync-battle.patch:44-48`) — so the "arity-1 = uid" premise is actively contradicted by the nearest evidence. If the read confirms a per-triangle id, it replaces the zone table; if it does not, nothing in the field changes. Rumble (0xF6-0xFC) stays out entirely.

Wall thud = latched px/pz unchanged for N ticks while a direction key is held — pure `B_KEY` + position deltas, no new path.

Ship-time preflight the field asserts, because an all-audio field fails SILENTLY otherwise: `Memoria.ini [Audio] PriorityToOGG=1` (custom spoken cues ride the loose-OGG override, `sound.py:1-12`) and a non-zero `SoundVolume` (`sound.py:173-185`), plus BGM off or floored so the cue channel is not masked.

Engine: stock, honestly — novel field, no fork gates, no DLL patch, config only. Budget: ~1-2 KB against a 0xFFFF entry cap (`binutils.py:39-53`). The one thing that must be bought before authoring is the `DoCalcOperationExt.cs:37-43` read, and after this restatement it buys an optimization, not the field.
