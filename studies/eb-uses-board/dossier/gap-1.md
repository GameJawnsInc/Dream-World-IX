Four substrate faults, two fatal. (1) THE EXPRESSIONS AS WRITTEN DO NOT EVALUATE. `exprsem.py:165` records `B_MEMBER` as **arity 0** — it pushes a Member *ref*, it does not read. A member READ must go through an extract op: the kit's own generator emits `B_SYSLIST[1] B_MEMBER(cur) B_SYSLIST[1] B_MEMBER(max) B_PICK const(N) B_DIV B_LT_E B_COUNT` (`battle/aiauthor.py:225`, the 56-boss shipping idiom). The pitch's `B_SYSLIST[1] B_MEMBER(36)` … `B_DIV` phase test leaves 3 values on the CalcStack and would be rejected by `exprsem.analyze`. Same for the read side of the read-modify-write on 140/141/142 — every `pos + 24` step needs a `B_PICK`. `B_BAFRAME` is **arity 1** (`exprsem.py:162`, DoCalcOperationExt.cs:21-26), not a nullary clock, so `B_SIN` over a bare `B_BAFRAME` is malformed too. Fixable, but it means the mechanism was never assembled, let alone run. (2) THE CADENCE DOES NOT EXIST WHERE THE PITCH PUTS IT. `battle-tuning.md:92-95` (load-bearing, in-game proven): "tag 1 = Main (**runs once at Init**)"; "a normal enemy turn dispatches to tag 7 (ATB), but **tag 7 only sets ATB timing** — the `Attack` command lives in tag 5". The engine's actual per-frame choreography lane is raw17 `btlseq` (~15 fps, `Wait`/anim/camera/move opcodes), which the kit already exposes as `[[scene.seq_patch|seq_replace|seq_insert]]` — and which is the ONLY other `SetCharacterData` caller, writing member 55/scale at `btlseq.cs:711`. ## 2. The Fight Writes The Ledger

**Status:** SURVIVED · **Effort:** medium · **Engine:** stock · **Round:** 2

**Pitch.** Enemy AI appends to a save-persisted vector as the fight unfolds — who died first, what phase you reached, how much HP the boss had left — and the field that catches you after the battle reads it back and narrates what actually happened instead of a generic victory line.

**Mechanism (as proposed).** The `0xD3 flexible_varfunc` token lives in the expression layer, which battle `.eb` shares verbatim, and `B_VECTOR (20,2)` is a resolvable LVALUE that is save-serialized through JsonParser — writing at `index == size` APPENDS, and a missing id at index 0 CREATES. So an enemy's tag-9 (Dying) body does `flex(20,2)[ledgerId, flex(21,1)[ledgerId]] <B_SYSLIST[1] B_MEMBER(41)> B_LET` to append its own level/id at the current size, and the tag-1 body appends a phase marker each transition. On return, the field's entry-0 tag-10 `Main_Reinit` — which a cloned/authored field must carry anyway or it softlocks after battle — reads `B_VECTOR_SIZE` to switch on how long the fight ran, and indexes rows for the details, feeding them into dialogue through `SetTextVariable 0x66` in its expression form (`arg_flags=0b10`, the only way past the ±32767 immediate). Teardown is `ClearMemoriaVector 0x11A` at the field's `[startup]`, and the seed idiom (`size←0` / `size←n`) stays law so a stale save can't replay someone else's fight. A cheap degenerate version needs no vector at all: the Dying body sets `Global.Bit[≥8712]` and the field branches on it.
