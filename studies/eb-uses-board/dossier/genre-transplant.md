## 1. The Ghost Racer

**Status:** SURVIVED · **Effort:** medium · **Engine:** stock · **Round:** 1

**Pitch.** A time-trial lane where your own previous best run walks beside you as a translucent second actor — recorded, save-persisted, and replayed from a gScriptVector. Racing genre, zero new engine surface.

**Mechanism (as proposed).** Record: the field's central ticker (one seated entry looping Wait(1)) samples the player every Nth tick via obj(uid=250).f[0] and .f[2] (verified live per-frame X/Z reads, content/behavior.py:2414-2423) and appends them to a vector with flex(20,2) B_VECTOR as an LVALUE — writing at index==size APPENDS, and gScriptVector is save-serialized through JsonParser, so the ghost survives the session. Replay: a second actor (own uid, SetModel + SetModelColor(0x8F) alpha for the translucent look) is driven by MoveInstantEx(0xBF, by uid, re-grounds) with EXPRESSION operands reading B_VECTOR(gid, 2*i) / B_VECTOR(gid, 2*i+1) — its argFlag bits make every coordinate computed. No pathing, no Walk, no WalkMesh.Collision cost beyond one extra mover. Split timing: B_FRAME (expr token 106) sampled at checkpoint tread regions (tag 2) rather than a tick counter, so it cannot drift against the 20fps logical tick. Best-lap comparison writes SetTextVariable(0x66) into a gMesValue slot. New best overwrites via B_LET at index i; a scrub is ClearMemoriaVector(0x11A). Budget: 2 ints per sample, 1 sample per 10 ticks, a 60s lap = 240 ints — trivial against the 0xFFFF offset budget since the data lives in the SAVE, not the .eb.

**Mechanism (screen correction).** Two fixes to the mechanism as pitched. (1) The translucency claim is the weak link: 0x8F SetModelColor takes FOUR u8 args (OP_ARG_COUNT[0x8F]==4, OP_ARG_SIZE [1,1,1,1]) and there is no kit helper or verified alpha semantics for it — treat it as an unverified RGB(+?) tint on the executing actor, not a proven alpha channel. Ground it against a real field's bytes first (grep the corpus with disasm for 0x8F call sites), and have a fallback look for the ghost (a distinct tint, or a different model) rather than betting the feature on alpha. (2) A per-tick MoveInstantEx teleport gives a foot-sliding ghost: drive a looping walk clip on the ghost actor (RunAnimation 0x40) alongside the position writes, and gate it to a run/idle pair off the per-sample delta so the replay reads as a runner. Also sample on B_FRAME deltas rather than assuming the 20fps logical tick equals wall time on the record side too, not just at checkpoints.

**Opcodes.** `0xBF MoveInstantEx`, `0x8F SetModelColor`, `0x66 SetTextVariable`, `0x11A ClearMemoriaVector`, `0x22 Wait`, `B_VECTOR flex(20,2)`, `B_VECTOR_SIZE flex(21,1)`, `B_FRAME`, `B_OBJSPECA obj(uid).f[]`

### Substrate verifier (engine expert) — survives

Checked every named primitive against the repo. The STORAGE + REPLAY core survives; four of the named opcodes/claims do not.

WHAT CHECKS OUT (no DLL needed):
- `0xBF MoveInstantEx` — `_optables.py` count 3, size [1,2,2]; `eb/opcodes.py:268` encodes it and sets `arg_flags` so x/z may be pre-encoded EXPRESSION blobs (mandatory here — the samples are computed). In-game proven (fort-condor rung-3 spawn-at-feet, `studies/fort-condor/PLAN.md:19,146`).
- `B_VECTOR flex(20,2)` / `B_VECTOR_SIZE flex(21,1)` — `eb/_exprtable.py:65-70`: a resolvable LVALUE, assignable via `B_LET`, index==size APPENDS, "both stores are SAVE-SERIALIZED". Stock Memoria (upstream `91e94a66`, an ancestor of the pinned base `6b8bb2d5` — `studies/behavior-trees/PLAN.md:613-618`); computed-index read AND write, VECTOR_SIZE seed/resize, OOB fail-soft all in-game proven on bench 30415. Persistence: `JsonParser.cs:521-545`; `gScriptVector.Clear()` sits in `EventEngine.Initialize` (New Game only, see `memoria-patches/s12-engine-edits.patch:41`), so it rides field changes and saves.
- Player position read — `obj(uid=250).f[0]`/`.f[2]` = world X/Z, shipped in the behavior compiler (`content/behavior.py:2414-2416`; field map at `content/platform.py:246`).
- `Wait(1)` per-frame ticker — `0x22`, count 1 size [1] (u8, max 255 frames); `opcodes.py:188`. The Wait(1) poll is the shipped "stock poll cadence" (`behavior.py:3765-3780`).
- `0x66 SetTextVariable` [1,2] exists; `GetTimerTime`/`ChangeTimerTime` are the proven time-trial clock (`studies/minigame-ui/SURVEY.md`).
- BUDGETS ARE NOT THE PROBLEM. Samples live in the vector, not in bytecode; the real ceiling the kit enforces is the u16 relative branch/switch offset (`eb/cmdasm.py:154-176`, function body >65535B unassemblable) and the ticker is a few hundred bytes. The 26-bit CalcStack (`(t0<<6)>>6`, EBin.cs:1683) killed Path B because of CROSS PRODUCTS; a world coord (±few thousand) and an index counter are nowhere near ±33,554,431.

WHAT IS REFUTED:
1. TRANSLUCENCY IS NOT REACHABLE — and the correction is wrong in both directions. `ff9mapkit/docs/OBJECT_CARRY.md:141` documents `0x8F SetModelColor (CHRCOLOR)` with **arg0 = uid** (also `eventscan.py:558` REF_OPS). So its four u8 args are (uid, R, G, B): it targets a NAMED actor (better than the critique claims) and has NO fourth channel to be alpha (fatal to the pitch's headline). Sweeping OP_NAMES for the whole transparency family leaves only `0xED SetTileLoopAlpha` (BG tiles) and `0xEC FadeFilter` (full-screen) — neither is per-actor. A translucent ghost is a DLL patch the idea does not admit to.
2. `RunAnimation 0x40` IS SELF-ONLY. The remote-actor op is `0xBD RunAnimationEx(uid, anim)` [1,2] (+ `0xBE WaitAnimationEx`). `0x98 MakeAnimationLoop` has NO Ex form, so a looping clip must be re-fired per cycle or run from the ghost's own entry. Repairable, but the named op is wrong.
3. `0xBF` IS THE WRONG MOVE FAMILY for anything but a strictly single-floor lane. `studies/field-coop/platforms-teleport.md:213,571`: `0x1D`/`0xBF` parse only X and Z and pass the literal `POS_COMMAND_DEFAULTY = 32768f` (`EventEngine.Constructor.cs:11`) to `SetActorPosition`, so the floor resolver always picks the TOPMOST overlapping triangle — "Do not use this opcode/pattern for a teleport... it silently picks the wrong floor". The Y-aware family is `0xA1`/`0xAD MoveInstantXZYEx(uid,x,z,y)` [1,2,2,2].
4. `B_FRAME` IS HAND-WAVED. `eb/exprsem.py:162` gives it arity **1** (it takes an operand) citing `DoCalcOperationExt.cs:21-26`, paired with `B_BAFRAME`, with no annotation of what the operand selects and zero shipped call sites in the kit. "Sample on B_FRAME deltas" is a law in a docstring. Worse, the "20fps logical tick" is a WORLD-MAP number (`ff9.world.OnUpdate20FPS`, `studies/path-d-new-world/walk-decode-claims.md:9,73`); field logic runs on `Configuration.Graphics.FieldTPS`, user-editable in Memoria.ini — this very install ships WorldTPS=28 and BattleTPS=15, i.e. non-default. A tick-indexed recording is calibrated to the recorder's FieldTPS and will not agree with a seconds-based `GetTimerTime` across installs.

TWO HAZARDS THE PITCH DOESN'T ADMIT:
5. THE SEED LAW COLLIDES WITH PERSISTENCE. Every kit vector table is re-seeded `size←0; size←n` at Main_Init precisely so stale saves stay inert (`content/behavior.py:2444-2452`; SURVEY.md: "keep it law"), and `gScriptVector` is ONE global id-keyed store shared by every field in the save. A ghost vector must be exempted from that seed AND claim an id outside the kit's auto band (`TABLE_ID_BASE = 1000`, `behavior.py:715`), or the next field that seeds a colliding id wipes the run.
6. ZERO VANILLA GROUNDING. Vanilla fields "rarely use" gScriptVector at all (`studies/battle-coop/state-mirror-lane.md:207`), and the project's own record already flags a per-tick vector loop as "a NEW bytecode shape (grounded nowhere in stock fields — needs the verbatim-first treatment)" (`studies/behavior-trees/PLAN.md:608-612`); the shipped vocabulary fires `MoveInstantEx` on SPAWN only. Per CLAUDE.md §7 that is a bench-first obligation, not a design detail — and §8's "the defect follows the authorship" applies squarely.

VERDICT: not refuted on substrate for the record/persist/replay core — every primitive exists, most are in-game proven, and it needs no DLL. Refuted on the feature's own name: "translucent" is unreachable without an engine patch, and the timing lane as specified is unproven.

**Sharpened version.** THE TINTED PACER (not "ghost" — drop the alpha claim from the name).

A single-floor time-trial lane records the player's path into a save-persisted `gScriptVector` and replays it on a second actor, on stock Memoria.

RECORD (one Wait(1) ticker entry, while user control is live):
- Per tick, append two cells: `size(px_tid)` index → `obj(uid=250).f[0]`, then `size(pz_tid)` → `.f[2]` — the append idiom (index == size) from `eb/_exprtable.py:65-70`, proven on bench 30415.
- Do NOT sample on `B_FRAME` (arity 1, semantics unannotated, no call site). The index IS the clock: N ticks of `Wait(1)`. Cap N hard in the ticker (e.g. 3600) so a lingering player cannot grow the save unbounded.
- Display time from `GetTimerTime` (seconds, in-game proven) and STORE the tick count separately; never mix the two clocks. State plainly that the pace is FieldTPS-relative: a ghost recorded on `Configuration.Graphics.FieldTPS = X` and replayed at Y races at X/Y wall speed. Stamp the recording's FieldTPS into cell 0 and refuse/flag a mismatched replay.
- Commit the run to the "best" vector only at the finish line, under a `size←0; size←n` rewrite, so an abandoned run leaves nothing.

REPLAY (the pacer's own entry, its own Wait(1) loop):
- Position: `0xAD MoveInstantXZYEx(uid, x, z, y)` [1,2,2,2], NOT `0xBF`. `0xBF` hardcodes `Y = POS_COMMAND_DEFAULTY = 32768f` and therefore resolves to the topmost overlapping triangle (`studies/field-coop/platforms-teleport.md:213,571`). On a genuinely flat, non-overlapping lane `0xBF` is acceptable — lint the walkmesh for XZ-overlapping floors and refuse the feature if any exist.
- Animation: `0xBD RunAnimationEx(uid, anim)`, gated on the per-sample delta to a run/idle pair. A teleported actor never triggers the engine's walk/idle auto-swap (see `opcodes.stop_animation`'s note: a player-cloned NPC "glides in the idle pose"), so the clip must be driven explicitly and re-fired each cycle — `0x98 MakeAnimationLoop` has no Ex form.
- Look: `0x8F SetModelColor(uid, R, G, B)` — a saturated single-channel TINT, verified as a uid-targeted CHRCOLOR (`docs/OBJECT_CARRY.md:141`), with NO alpha. Ground it first by disassembling real `0x8F` call sites in the HW corpus (`C:\gd\FFIX\reference\test2\`) to learn the range convention before trusting a value. If the tint reads badly, the fallback is a DIFFERENT MODEL (a silhouette/armor variant), not an engine patch. Any true translucency is an admitted DLL change and belongs on the `memoria-patches/` stack, out of scope for a "zero new engine surface" pitch.

VECTOR HYGIENE (the one real new surface):
- Reserve a ghost band well outside the kit's auto-allocation band (`TABLE_ID_BASE = 1000`), document it as globally owned, and EXEMPT those ids from the compiler's Main_Init `size←0; size←n` seed — otherwise the seed law that keeps stale saves inert is exactly what erases the feature. `0x11A ClearMemoriaVector` (1 operand, 3 bytes, zero shipped call sites) is the reset verb; bench it before trusting it.

BENCH ORDER (verbatim-first, one mechanism per playtest, per CLAUDE.md §7): (1) append 2 cells/tick for 10s, read them back after a save/load and a field round trip; (2) replay position only onto a static-posed actor — does it slide smoothly or stutter at FieldTPS; (3) add `0xBD` clip gating; (4) add the tint; (5) only then the race framing. Budget is a non-issue (the bytecode is a few hundred bytes against a 65535B per-function branch span; values are ~1e3 against a 26-bit CalcStack) — the risks are per-tick cost, vector id hygiene, and the fact that stock FF9 ships no field that does any of this.
