**Date:** 2026-08-04 · **Scope:** how the toolkit interfaces with the 3D overworld — data structures, math, pipelines, tooling, validation. Not the field/battle pillars except by contrast.

**Method:** staged multi-agent audit run as an external 3D-game-technology consultancy would. 4 recon scouts built a shared dossier ([DOSSIER.md](DOSSIER.md)); 6 practice-area experts (geometry pipeline, tools UX, procedural content, spatial math, validation/QA, engine integration) produced 40 findings against real code ([RAW-FINDINGS.md](RAW-FINDINGS.md), summaries in [EXPERT-SUMMARIES.md](EXPERT-SUMMARIES.md)); the 40 findings were clustered into 15 themes and each cluster was adversarially verified by an independent skeptic that re-read the cited code and attempted refutation ([VERDICTS.md](VERDICTS.md)); a completeness critic then hunted for what all six lenses missed and found 5 more, two of them measured with scripts run against this worktree and the live install ([CRITIC.md](CRITIC.md)). **No cluster was refuted; 5 were confirmed outright, 10 confirmed with corrections; every verdict at high confidence.** Several expensive sub-proposals were refuted during verification and are recorded in "Refused by verification" below so they are not re-proposed.

## The diagnosis in one paragraph

The geometry core is professional-grade and measurably clean: one write seam (`mesh.deploy_override`), unindexed-contract-by-construction, an engine-rejected-triangle rate on custom Terrain *below* stock's own (1.1% vs 4.1% across 1,246 deployed files re-derived independently), and the single best gate in the codebase — `transplant.py`'s *differential* MISS census, which encodes the compare-against-the-neighbour law the project paid thirteen playtests to learn. The disease is not the mesh math; it is that **the toolkit cannot see, and its verdicts never touch actuals**. The kit authors a textured 3D world and ships zero ability to render a triangle — the only calibrated engine-faithful renderer is a study script hardcoded to one bench, and 25 study scripts each carry their own rasterizer. Every instrument with a demonstrated predictive record is study-local; the shipped verbs run weaker gates or none (`world-terrain` writes stock-land displacement with no check of any kind); one shipped gate is structurally unable to fire (`interior.census_gate` scores an uncut Sea4 the island lane never deploys); and nothing offline ever reads back what the engine actually built, rejected, or holds on disk. The verified fixes are therefore almost entirely **promotion and wiring, not invention** — nearly every capability recommended below already exists in this repository, tested, in a file only one site can use.

## What is genuinely best-practice — protect it, do not refactor it

- The single write seam + its two unskippable low-layer guards (`mesh.py:71-74` unindexed contract, `mesh.py:43-57` off-grid refusal). Named by the critic as "the healthiest code in the package."
- The differential MISS census (`transplant.py:2937-2969`) — the template every other gate should copy.
- `discmirror.auto_mirror`'s EVIDENCE CONTRACT — derives everything from actual deploy-write return values; the template for post-edit hooks (rec 8).
- `meshedit.py` — laws enforced at call sites with their motivating falsifications recorded inline, 100% offline-covered; the promotion template for rec 1.
- The cell-tag family (`entrance.py:47-79`) and the single-sourced 24×20 grid — model coordinate-convention ownership.
- The research-side practice itself: registered predictions, calibrate-before-judging, blind-spot ledgers, adversarial self-refutation. Better than most AAA teams; the problem is transfer to the kit, not rigor.

## Ranked recommendations

Ordering = impact on the ability to author the 3D world correctly and quickly, per verified effort, with sequencing dependencies honored. Every item has an exact first step and acceptance criteria in [VERDICTS.md](VERDICTS.md) (by cluster key) or [CRITIC.md](CRITIC.md) (by number).

### Tier 1 — give the toolkit eyes (do these first)

**1. Promote the offline renderer into the kit: `world/render.py` + `world-render --around WX,WZ`.** *(impact 4, medium — clusters `offline-renderer` + `promotion-path`)*
The panel's unanimous top item and the axis with the receipts: 8 rounds chasing a ground-junction defect with zero images taken, and after the render gate came online the next three owner verdicts all passed — the only regime change in the 13-verdict arc. The lift is cheap because `render_gate.py` already runs entirely on kit APIs and is already load-bearing infrastructure at the wrong address (`terrain_gate.py` and 5 probes import it); this *deletes* surface by replacing 25 per-study rasterizers with one. The one genuinely new part is the auto camera rig, which must reproduce the two vantage classes (near-top-down ~20u, low offshore graze ~3u) that hand-placed cameras needed to catch playtest-8 — register that as a falsifiable prediction. Warn-by-default, blind-spot ledger printed in output, opt-in `--render` on writers only, never default-on. Acceptance: byte-identical PNGs vs the committed bench output.

**2. Spatial index for `placement.place` — the fix that makes every other gate affordable.** *(impact 4, small — CRITIC #2, measured)*
The critic benchmarked the pure-Python linear scan: ~2s per census on one 4.6k-tri mesh, and a real gate run is minutes (full 8-part registration stack, MISSes scan to completion, region censuses scale with area). Every "sample finer / gate more chokepoints" prescription in this report multiplies that cost. Bucketing triangles into a 4u XZ grid and taking the minimum buffer index among candidate-bucket passers is *exactly* semantics-preserving (the engine rule is first-in-buffer-order, not nearest-hit), testable as byte-identical output, ~40 lines, ~2 orders of magnitude. Do this before or alongside recs 3–4.

**3. Port the stacked-sheet walkability verdict into `placement.py` and gate on it.** *(impact 4, small — cluster `walkability-oracle`)*
The kit's only shipped walkability gate returns first-hit-or-MISS, so it is structurally blind to the one defect class with a playtest receipt: a walkable sheet under a walkable sheet (the lawn-under-hill pin, reproduced at 0.00u by ~60 lines of `walk_sim.all_sheets` + census). Do **not** promote the 421-line movement engine (it produced zero real defects and three false accusations when run island-wide) — port `all_sheets` + `WALK_OK`, add `stacked`/`inversions` keys to `census()`, gate at the three sites that already gate on `miss`, add the 0x31EE veto and a registration-order-exception refusal to `place()`. First step is a single red test: lawn tri emitted before a carried tri 1.5u above it, `census()["stacked"]` non-empty.

**4. Fix the census: revive the dead gate, then seam pairs, then the differential everywhere.** *(impact 4, small — cluster `census-blindness`)*
`interior.census_gate` — the gate for `world-forest`/`hill`/`mountain` — censuses against an UNCUT full-cell Sea4 plane while the island lane deploys a CUT one, making its `MISS == 0` predicate unreachable for the terrain-hole class it exists to catch; and a study ledger already banks "census_gate did not raise" as a pass signal. The fix is a one-line `_cut_plane` swap plus the falsification test that does not exist. Then: lift the 0.5u-stride seam micro-census into `census(seam=...)` (block seams are where cross-block coverage gaps live and no gate samples them), and promote the differential census to `placement.census_delta` — only after which may sampling pitch rise (at finer pitch without the differential, four stock blocks go red on their own bytes). Also state the measured detection floor in the docstring: a one-triangle hole is missed ~25% of the time at default pitch. A law in a docstring is a wish.

**5. Confirm the ~1s world iteration loop and fix the lying relaunch notes.** *(impact 3, small + one playtest — cluster `small-frictions`)*
The scene rebuild re-reads every loose override from disk ("exit to field and return", or ~ → World → Reload overworld on state), yet two CLI handlers and an example README claim loose assets aren't hot-reloaded. One shared `_print_world_apply_note()` across ~25 world verbs, a grep-level drift test, and one playtest (deploy a visible hill, reload, check `[WorldMeshOverride] loaded` in Memoria.log). If confirmed, every future world iteration drops from relaunch+walk-back to ~1 second — the cheapest iteration-speed win available anywhere in this report.

### Tier 2 — make the write seam safe and the verdicts touch actuals

**6. A deploy ledger, overwrite refusal, and lifecycle verbs at the write seam.** *(impact 4, small→medium — clusters `provenance-manifest` + `declarative-composition` rung 1 + CRITIC #4; three independent lenses converged here)*
Today a deployed world is 20MB of unattributable bytes on state 18+ concurrent sessions share, with no enumerate, no revert, no lock, unconditional overwrite, and a version field nothing checks — and the project has already lost authored work to a silent overwrite (Grimhorn's falls). All at the one write seam, keeping mesh bytes untouched: (a) backup-on-differing-overwrite (`.ff9mesh.bak-<ts>`, plus the `fuse._existing_overrides` extension filter — that hazard is live today); (b) append-only JSONL ledger `{cell, part, discs, sha256, kit_version, utc, argv}`; (c) refuse to overwrite bytes matching no ledger entry ("another session or a hand edit owns these bytes; `--force-overwrite`"); (d) `world-ledger [--drift]` / `world-status` / `world-revert --since` read verbs; (e) reject a bad version magic on read. Reconciliation, not quality scoring — which is what keeps it out of the 0-of-13 class.

**7. Unify the Sea5 tile classifier — three copies, three arity rules, live on the current HEAD arc.** *(impact 4, small — CRITIC #3)*
The same "which deepset/transition tile is this cell?" question has three answers: `water.read_sea5_tiles` requires all 4 corners, `transplant._sea5_deepsets` requires ≥3 + a degenerate-UV guard, `rimretile._sea5_deepsets` requires nothing — and `_fit_tile` fed one corner returns an essentially arbitrary rotation which the rim retiler then iterates to a fixed point. This is the exact shape of "the audit is green and the owner sees a seam," on the pillar HEAD (`972918db`) is actively fixing. One classifier `water.classify_sea5_cell(corners, *, min_corners=4)`, both copies call it, arity relaxation becomes a named argument, plus a corpus test that the three call paths agree.

**8. A `post_edit` hook: geometry edits must restamp or warn about the systems they invalidate.** *(impact 4, small→medium — CRITIC #1)*
Six lenses all audited the producer side; nobody asked what is a *function of* the geometry. The disc-mirror is auto-run in every writer — the generalization stopped there. Vehicle legality (`coastnav`) auto-runs for exactly one verb of the several that can put land over water (its own docstring names the sail-through-cliff hazard `world-reclaim`/`world-terrain` produce); the minimap is never auto-refreshed by anything; encounters aren't even joined (a new landmass mints topographs with no report of which of the 355 records its cells resolve to). One `post_edit(written, ...)` beside `auto_mirror` (which already receives the written cell set — the hard part): coastnav restamp for land-over-water verbs, staleness warnings for minimap and the (zone, topograph) join. Cheapest 80%: even just printing the reminder in the four handlers that print nothing.

**9. Honest gate headlines + promote the two calibrated texture gates + the one real coverage hole.** *(impact 3, small — cluster `gate-wiring`)*
This is an honesty defect, not a coverage defect — do **not** build a new gate battery (that mints exactly the surface DEFECT-FOLLOWS-AUTHORSHIP warns about). (a) `world-transplant`/`world-island` print "gates CLEAN" in the same breath as warn rows — derive the headline from a real pass/warn/fail status, with a test asserting "CLEAN" never prints over a warn. (b) `zero_uv_area_gate` + `family_rect_gate` are measured at 1.000 clean on stock — flip them to enforce-by-default. (c) The one unguarded verb: `terrain.reshape` displaces stock land with zero checks; reuse `interior.GATE_CLIMB`/`MAX_FLANK` over its moved-vert set (the climb class is adjacent to the save-brick). (d) Fix the overclaiming `weld_audit gates every build` docstring.

**10. Close the engine boundary: transcribe the loader's predicates, pin them against drift, spend the read-back instrument.** *(impact 3, medium — cluster `engine-boundary`)*
The kit MODELS the engine instead of MEASURING it. (1) `validate_blockmesh(bm)` raising ValueError on the five predicates transcribed from the s34 patch's ReadMesh + a NaN/inf scan, called from `write_ff9mesh` — no degenerate-tri gate (the WALL LAW records that filter misfiring). (2) A test asserting the patch text still carries the literals, so an engine-side bump fails the suite instead of voiding deploys; collapse the three duplicate header parsers. (3) `world-readback <dump-dir>`: the s22 debug-menu mesh dump ships today with zero consumers — ingest it and diff engine-actual vs deployed vert-for-vert, walkmesh vs render mesh, and the actually-bound material (the empirical answer the EFFECTIVE-PREFAB law is currently guessing at). Human-in-the-loop like game_snap. (4) At the *next* DLL rebuild only: make a rejected override on reclaimed sea fall back to SEA with a log marker, not to a different real block's walkable geometry at the wrong coordinates, unlogged — the nastiest failure mode found in the audit.

### Tier 3 — coherence, knowledge in code, hygiene

**11. One engine-cited `REGISTRATION_ORDER` + feed `census_gate` the real carried parts.** *(impact 3, small — cluster `part-order`)* The engine's part-registration order is hardcoded four times in three mutually inconsistent orders. Export one cited constant + `build_meshlist()` in `placement.py`, convert the three real call sites. The fix that buys a playtest: `interior.census_gate` currently omits the real carried Object/Falls/River parts, so a carried massif Object over the probe point passes a gate it should fail.

**12. `frames.py` — additive only, with the constants-drift test written first.** *(impact 3, small — cluster `coordinate-centralization`)* `64.0` appears as a bare literal 83 times in `transplant.py` alone; the 4u lattice index exists in two incompatible Z-sign conventions under the same name; exactly two modules know the world is toroidal, each with a private extent, one wrapping only X. Step 1 is a ~30-line test asserting the 14 literals agree (zero refactor risk — either proves consistency or finds live drift today). Step 2: additive `frames.py` with the sign encoded in the *name* (`lattice_ij` vs `lattice_raw_xz`). Step 3: make `terrain.reshape`'s silent seam-drop loud. **Migration of existing call sites is refused** — new code imports it; old sites convert only when already being edited.

**13. Make the invocable surface carry the project's verdicts: falsified-lane banners + `donors.toml`.** *(impact 3, small — cluster `knowledge-in-code`)* `world-island --beach` was falsified over 4 playtests yet ships as an uncaveated flag whose name collides with the *live* `world-transplant --beach-mint`. Banner in `--help` + runtime note + a test that goes red if the banner is removed; rename the dead lane in CLAUDE.md §8 to kill the collision. Plus a read-only `world/data/donors.toml` (the field pillar already has its catalog) with a drift test tying it to the frozen literals — resolving the README's self-contradiction while transcribing.

**14. A differential T-junction gate in transplant.** *(impact 3, small — cluster `grammar-as-code`)* The kit's own tested `find_tjunctions` has zero production callers. Wire it beside the weld-audit gate, differential (`new_count == 0` vs pre-edit bytes), scoped to edited tris + one ring (it's O(verts×edges) — whole-block is unrunnable). **Refused:** the `grammar.toml` percentile table — encoding marginals is the 0-of-13 mode for a lane already declared closed; the one grammar law worth code is the zero-freedom BAND-CONTINUATION check, landed as an assert when that lane reopens.

**15. Split the DCC bridge: fail-closed writes, permissive reads, deployed-bytes inspection.** *(impact 3, small — cluster `dcc-bridge`)* The OBJ round-trip destroys per-triangle IDALL on terrain (uniform-retag walkability hazard no gate can see) — *refuse* terrain rebuilds rather than adding a sidecar; add a block-frame bbox gate before deploy. Then open the half that buys speed: `world-mesh-export --mod-folder` reading the deployed stack (`read_block_stacked`) with all parts + per-part materials, so an author can finally open their own island in Blender. OBJ+MTL, not glTF.

**16. **17. **18. Two-line `tris`/`flat_index` coherence assert at the write seam. Hypothesis properties on `meshedit` only (the ring-conformity class shipped a real missing face past a sampled gate); do not extend into transplant/coastmorph.

## Refused by verification — do not re-propose without new evidence

Recorded in the project's dead-end spirit; each was proposed by an expert and killed by a skeptic with reasons (full arguments in [VERDICTS.md](VERDICTS.md)):

- **A new `world/gates.py` battery wired into all builders** — mints authored surface against the 12/13 law; the payload was honesty, not coverage.
- **`grammar.toml` percentile tables** — scoring against marginals, the 0-of-13 mode, for a closed lane.
- **Promoting `walk_sim.py`/`coast_lint.py` wholesale** — the 421-line engine confirmed but did not discover; island-wide it produced 0 real defects and 3 false accusations.
- **Default-on renders per deploy / golden-image build gates / fail-on-pixel-diff** — every world edit changes pixels; the arc's own gates needed allowlists to stop being permanently red. Warn + opt-in `--enforce`, always.
- **Embedding provenance in the `.ff9mesh` format** — breaks the byte-determinism contract a dozen tests and the mirror rely on.
- **A per-face IDALL sidecar through Blender** — Blender doesn't guarantee face order; a mis-applied IDALL is an invisible walkability bug, a refusal is free.
- **Routing OBJ n-gons through `earclip`** — 2D clipper, re-winds the ring; would collapse vertical faces and flip winding (SEA-SHEET LAWS).
- **Migrating the ~12 inline world→block sites / consolidating the 6 PIP copies** — no behavioral divergence today; touching playtest-load-bearing arithmetic is the defect factory. Directional rule instead.
- **"Run at two sites → promote or DELETE" study policy** — 9 world modules cite studies/ paths 27 times as contracts; a promote-quota mints surface on a schedule.
- **Flipping `--no-normals` default** — the channel is free, the loader binds it harmlessly, and Object/Sea materials were never verified. Fix the lying print string only.
- **A stock-baseline quality-audit verb / Memoria.log-scraping `world-doctor`** — quality scoring against stock marginals is the 0-of-13 class; read-back (rec 10) answers the same questions from ground truth.
- **File locking on the shared mod folder** — the ledger + ownership refusal gets the concurrency win without a lock protocol.

## Suggested sequencing

Tier 1 is three small sessions plus one medium (renderer) and one playtest (reload). Recs 2→3→4 share `placement.py` and compound: index makes density affordable, stacked-sheets and seam census make it *see*. Rec 6 before any further composition work (it is the precondition rung of rec 16). Recs 7, 11, 13 are each a single commit-sized session suitable for gap-filling. Per the project's own one-change-per-test law, none of these should share a playtest with content work.
