# Rung A1: picking

**Status:** built and in-game proven 2026-10-23 on `claude/terrain-shaping-continue-4647b2`
(`20261023-111215-a1-reach` and three `world-teleport` runs, all predictions held). The owner's use in the app is
pending.

## What was built

- **The pick:** `worldview3d.pick` casts the eye ray through the clicked pixel (`pixel_ray`, carrying the same
  half-pixel as the clip matrix) against every packed triangle, vectorised Moller-Trumbore, front faces only (the view
  culls back faces, so you never pick one you cannot see). The edge arrays are cached per part on first use.
- **In the view:** a left click that wanders under 4 device px picks (the camera holds still meanwhile); past that it
  is a drag and orbits. The picked point gets a pin: two crossed quads in the theme accent over a dark halo, drawn
  on top.
- **`world/pointinfo.py`:** what is at the point, each fact an existing kit answer:
  - the triple (world wrapped / block / 32u cell);
  - the engine's ground there: `placement.place`, the sky ray, over the block's parts as `render.cell_meshes`
    resolves them. That is the one per-cell resolution `load_batches` now also draws through, so the query reads
    what was drawn. Then topograph, area, entrance event, walkable on foot, the canopy sink;
  - every sheet on the vertical, warned by the census's own stacked-sheet rule (two WALK_OK sheets
    STACK_GAP_MIN apart; "LOWER" when the engine grounds you under the top one). Land over Sea4 is listed, not
    warned;
  - who the game takes each file from: the first `FolderNames` folder holding it, a part hidden by a higher folder,
    and a shown folder missing from `FolderNames`;
  - the deploy ledger's last row for the block (verb, date);
  - the nearest navipos landmark with its distance.
- **The World tab:** a pick selects its block on the chart and fills the details strip (worker thread). The
  copy button becomes "Copy position" (the canonical pair, 2 dp). The second slot becomes **Teleport game here**:
  a dev-checkout-only job, `tools/world_teleport.py X Z`, which attaches to the running game through the harness,
  sends the engine's overworld teleport, waits for the settle, reads the position back and disarms. The shipped
  package never imports the harness, the Build tab's `deploy_field.py` precedent.

## The in-game check (registered in `a1_prep.py` before the game opened)

`a1_prep.py` asked `point_info` at all 54 navipos landmarks. 15 qualify: walkable, modelled, and no part hidden by
another folder (the view must draw what the game binds there, P4). It took three far apart and made each click
exactly as the tab makes it: the live scene (all 480 blocks, 287,319 tris, 5.2 s), an orbit camera on the marker,
the marker's pixel, and `pick` at its centre. `a1_run.py` then reached the world (bench 6603, the terrain rounds'
route), ran the button's own argv for each pick, and quit the game.

| landmark | P1 pick to marker | P2 read-back x,z to pick (tol 0.05) | P3 read-back y to stand_y (tol 0.15) | pick time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evil Forest (on canopy) | 0.000u | 0.001 | 3.621 vs 3.624 (-0.003) | 79 ms (first, builds the cache) |
| Landing Site | 0.000u | 0.004 | 4.285 vs 4.286 (-0.001) | 46 ms |
| Chocobo's Lagoon | 0.000u | 0.003 | 6.414 vs 6.414 (-0.0004) | 27 ms |

All held. The Evil Forest still shows the party on the forest canopy, as its predicted canopy stand height said.
P1 at 0.000u is partly by construction: the marker is projected at `point_info`'s ground height, so the pick only had
to hit the drawn surface where the ground query stands, which it did to float precision. The stills stay in the
gitignored run folders (game renders are not committed, `ff9mapkit/docs/PROVENANCE.md`); the two JSON records are in
`evidence/a1/`.

## Tests

- `tests/test_world_pointinfo.py`, 8 tests, hermetic (synthetic BlockMeshes through `cell_meshes`, a tmp stack):
  the triple and its wrap; the ground as the engine's first hit (Object before Terrain); Sea4 under land is not a
  stack; an Object lawn under Terrain is an inversion; the canopy stand height; MISS; a higher folder hiding the
  shown part; a folder missing from `FolderNames`; the ledger's verb.
- `tests/test_worldview3d.py`, 7 more:
  - a pick at pixel (i, j)'s centre lands on the point `render.project` puts on (i, j) (40 random pixels, odd size);
  - nearest front face, through a back face, sky is None;
  - click versus drag through `QApplication.sendEvent`;
  - the pin geometry;
  - the strip, copy and teleport wiring;
  - no teleport button without the dev tool;
  - a superseded point read never overwrites a newer pick.
- **Mutations:** drop the ray's half-pixel, 1 red; drop the front-face filter, 1 red.
