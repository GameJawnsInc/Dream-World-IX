# C10: placing across the world's x seam

**Status:** BUILT + IN GAME, 2026-10-24 (`c10_lab.py`: a hill and a forest centred just west of x = 0, through the
World tab's cards; in game both slopes of the hill, one in column 23 and one in column 0, stood at the predicted
height). Owner request: "Go on with the seam: make the relief verbs seat a placement that straddles x = 0, so the
editor can place across the map's wrap."

## What was already true, and what was not

**The relief verbs were seam-aware already** (the seam rung, 2026-10-20, `test_world_seam_wrap.py`):
`interior.read_deployed_blocks` reads an UNWRAPPED window round the point (columns -1, 0, 1 keep their keys; each
reads its wrapped label's file), the carve and its gates run in one contiguous frame, and the labels wrap back once,
at the write. Measured again before this rung, on drafts of FF9CustomMap-world: `world-hill` and `world-forest`
centred at x -6 both seat, all gates clean, writing columns 23 and 0. The plan's "relief verbs are not seam-aware"
line was stale.

**The editor was not:**
- **The cards read the island in [0, 1536).** Their soup (`CarryTool._read_soup`, `WHOLE_MAP`) is cut at x = 0, so a
  ghost there met no ground under its west half: the cheap laws refused what the verb would seat (C1's Not here).
- **The 3D view drew column 23 at the far end of the map**, 1536u from column 0, so an island across the seam
  showed as two halves at opposite edges.

## What it does now

- **Two soups per folder, two frames.** Each card reads the island twice: in [0, 1536) as before, and across x = 0
  (`SEAM_MAP`: the same 24 columns, columns 12-23 west of it, unwrapped, as the verbs' own window keys them). Both are
  read whenever the card reads the island, so a click near the seam never waits. A centre within `SEAM_BAND` (384u)
  of x = 0 is judged in the SEAM FRAME, where neither frame's own cut (x = 0/1536 east, x = +-768 seam) comes within
  384u of the shape.
- **`center` is always folded into [0, 1536)**: the command, the recipe row, the verdict match and the labels name
  the folded point. The ghost and the cheap laws use `fcenter()`, the centre as the frame spells it (x 1534 is -2 in
  the seam frame). The hill, the forest and the mountain cards use it; the island card (no soup) and the sink, coast,
  carry and river cards (their own clicks) are unchanged.
- **The view draws the seam twice** (`worldview3d.seam_copies`): the triangles within 192u (three columns) of x = 0
  again across it, columns 0-2 east of x = 1536 and columns 21-23 west of x = 0. So an island across the seam reads
  whole at BOTH ends of the map. A pick on a copy is folded back by the World tab. A drawing near the seam (the ghost,
  its rim, the refusal marks, a picked step's pin) is drawn at both ends too (`seam_shifts`), and the ghost drags
  from either.
- A card handed one injected soup (the tests', the snaps') has no seam frame and keeps to the east one, so a bench at
  x 1214 reads as it always did.

**THE ONE SPELLING (found by the lab, a kit fix).** The verbs read their window round the point AS GIVEN: x -2 and
x 1534 are one point, but the first read the island in a frame 1536u from the second's, and its float rounding wrote
other bytes at the seam (on real bytes: `Block[0][6] Terrain`, one file of six, after a hill and a forest). The CLI's
`_parse_world_point` (world-hill, world-forest, world-mountain) and `world-fuse`'s `_row_point` now fold the point into
the mapped window first (`frames.wrap_world_xz`), the island lane's own law (`island.landmass` canonicalizes its
centre). A point inside the window is unchanged, so no other command writes anything different.
