# C12: every placed step can be edited -- the sink, the coast morph and the river move

**Status:** BUILT, 2026-10-24 (`c12_lab.py`, real bytes). Owner request: "Go on with edit cards for sink, coast and
river, so every placed step can be picked, retuned and put back like the relief shapes."

## What changed

C9 opened forest, mountain, hill, island and carry steps in their own cards; a sink, a coast morph and a river move
could only be deleted. Now each opens too, and `recipe.EDITABLE_TABLES` is every table the editor writes:

| step | the card opens on | change it by |
|---|---|---|
| sink | its island, picked again from the island list, and its two checkboxes | clicking another island; the checkboxes |
| coast morph | its block, the window its span names (matched exactly), its verb and depth | another window or verb; the depth |
| river move | its placement among the river's fitting shifts | clicking another free placement |

**THE CARDS WAIT FOR THEIR READS.** Each of the three reads something before it can say a row: the sink its island
list (minutes, once per install), the coast the block's lawful windows (seconds, cached), the river its shape (about
fifteen seconds, once per card). `CarryTool` holds the row (`_pending_row`) and seats it when the read comes in
(`_seat_row`); the river's `move_from` is held to a second read (where the river stands in the draft) when that one
comes in.

**THE SAID-BACK LAW.** A card opens on a step only if its handles build the step's row again, key for key
(`CarryTool._said_back`, for every card, the C9 ones included): an edit that changes nothing must change nothing. A
row they cannot say refuses before the card opens, or, once a held read comes in, cancels the open edit with the
reason (`on_cancel(note)`) and the draft goes back exactly. Before, a hand-written hill centre off the 0.1u grid
would have opened rounded, moving the shape before the author touched a handle.

## Found on the way

- **A click on open sea with the sink card open recursed without end.** The card re-placed a click it thought had
  come before the island list, but the test was "no island picked", which open sea also is; placing asked for the
  list, the cached list called back, and so on. It now re-places only a click that really came first.
- **The coast card read once per block which folders hold it.** After its own Accept, an undo or an edit lifting the
  morph out, the block still read as edited, so the card refused the morph it had just made (the lab's first E2). It
  reads again whenever the draft moves.
- **A river step's pin and pick pointed at the wrong place.** A `[[river]]` row's `at` is the carry's translation, not
  a place on the map, but `recipe.anchor` used it as one. A river step now has no anchor: a click on any block it
  changed picks it, and no pin is drawn.
- **The coast windows' tie.** The nearest-window pick ran again on every handle change, and a window listed earlier
  at the same distance could take over the held one. `coastedit.nearest(prefer=)` keeps the held window on a tie, and
  the card opens with its centre on the window's own run (`coastedit.anchor`).
- "The 1 step after it are back on top": the bar's counts now agree with their verbs.
