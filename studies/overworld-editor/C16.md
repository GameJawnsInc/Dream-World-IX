# C16: refusals that point at the problem

**Status:** BUILT, 2026-10-24. Owner request: "Go on with refusals that point at the problem: make the world verbs'
refusals carry the failing point or edge, so the 3D view marks where a shape was refused instead of only printing a
sentence." The plan's habit 2 ("make refusals say where").

## What was measured first

Every card already draws a refusal's `where` as pins on the map; the question was which refusals the cards actually
MEET carry one. The real cards, headless and synchronous, an in-process runner, a fresh draft of the live
FF9CustomMap-world: random handles at random lawn spots, Check only where the card's own cheap laws pass (the only
spots a card ever checks), 30 checks per relief card; and every donor the carry card lists, each at turn 0 and 90.

|---|---|---|
| hill | 5, 0 | 5, 0 |
| forest | 5, 0 | 5, 0 |
| mountain | 28, **21** | 28, **0** |
| carry | 12 (all `land-fit`, marked at the block's middle) | 0 dry runs refused: the 2 donors that always failed are refused at once, their edges marked |

## What changed

**The mountain's exact-weld law checked the whole read window.** 16 of the 21 unmarked mountain refusals were
"kept grass at (x, y, z) has two float positions -- a weld split before the zip", at the same few points wherever
the mountain went: (1456, -484), (-16, -384), (1460, -384), (187.129, -1088), sub-millimetre splits (2.6e-5 to
3e-4u) in the live folder between old writers. But the zip welds only to its hole ring's points. The check now
covers only the ring's keys (`interior.carve_mountain`); a split ON the ring still refuses, now marked (gate `weld`).
A mountain that passed before keeps the same weld floats (the ring keys see the same first float, in the same order).

**Bare refusals became located** (`interior.CarveRefused`: same message, so the CLI's text is byte-identical; the
points ride beside it):
- the ring accounting (`chain_ring`, `chain_rings`): the odd-degree points (gate `ring`);
- the geometry gate: now also each down-facing tri and each near-miss weld (it marked only annulus cracks and the
  longest edge);
- the bank skip law: each flipped tri (gate `skip`); the plug chart: its plug tris (`plug`); ensemble parts leaving
  the span (`span`); new tris sampling transparent or gutter atlas texels (`atlas`); and "no deployed island near"
  (`footprint`).

**THE LAND-FIT LAW, from the donor alone** (`transplantedit.too_wide`, in the carry card's cheap laws): the carry's
`land-fit` gate needs 2u of sea between its land (every Terrain and beach tri, the seabed included) and the rect's
frame, so land wider than the rect less 4u along either axis can never fit, whatever the target or shift. Two of the
card's listed donors are blocks cut from a continent's coast and fill their frame (desert-beach (20,5), sandy-coast
(18,15)): every check of them refused, after ten seconds, marked at the block's middle. The card now says at once
"Not this donor: its land runs to its block's edge (...)" and marks the two edges it runs to. It is a consequence of
the gate, so it refuses nothing the gate would pass.

**The carry's report marks a failing gate's own points.** `land-fit` names the sides of its land too near the frame
(`transplant._land_fit_gate`), and `_morph_report_json` marks those instead of the block's middle (a gate that names
no points keeps the middle). The printed gate lines leave `where` out, so the CLI's text is unchanged.

The cards name the new gates (`ring`, `weld`, `atlas`, `skip`, `plug`). `gui_snap world:3d-transplant` gains
`-wide`: the refused donor, its edges marked.
