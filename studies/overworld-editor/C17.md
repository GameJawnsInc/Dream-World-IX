# C17: the mountain card stops offering spots that will fail

**Status:** BUILT, 2026-10-24. Owner request: "Go on with the mountain card's cheap laws: they pass spots its real
gates refuse 28 of 30 times (apron slope, geometry, zip). Make the preview catch what it cheaply can, so the card
stops offering spots that will fail."

## The measure

A labelled sample through the real card (`c17_mtn_sample.py`): headless, an in-process runner, a fresh draft of the
live FF9CustomMap-world, random handles at random lawn spots, Check only where the card's own preview passes; at each,
the cheap features the card holds beside the gate's verdict. Then each candidate law was replayed over the very same
placements (`c17_preview_check.py`) and kept only if it refused NO placement the carve passes.

| | offered spots that pass | the refusals |
|---|---|---|
| before (C16) | 22 of 150 (15%) | geometry 46, apron 42, weld 15, ring 13, zip 12 |
| the apron gate fixed (same 150) | 61 of 150 | geometry 46, weld 15, ring 13, zip 12, probe 2, apron 1 |
| + the new preview (same 150) | 61 of 118 offered (52%) | 32 refused before any check, none of them a pass |
| a fresh sample, the final card | **76 of 150 (51%)** | geometry 52, zip 16, probe 3, apron 2, census 1; all 74 marked |

## What changed

**THE APRON SLOPE judged the whole span** (`interior.carve_mountain`). The gate meant for the grass the apron lifts
measured every plain tri of the span, so steep ground the lift never moved -- a hill's flank, a vertical curtain,
36 to 107u from the mountain, the same few tris again and again (31.1 degrees sixteen times, 90 sixteen times) --
refused every mountain whose span reached it: all 42 of the sample's apron refusals. It now judges the tris the lift
moved (a vertex in ``lift_of``). 39 of the 42 pass; the rest refuse on later gates, or on a lifted tri that really is
too steep. Bytes are unchanged for any mountain that passed before: the gate only decides.

**THE HOLE RING, read before the carve** (`mountainedit.foot_ring`, in the card's preview). The carve drops every
tri with a vertex inside the rim or within ``MTN_CLEAR`` of it -- a plan-only test, since the apron's lift moves
heights only -- and zips the rim to the points the dropped tris share with the kept ones. The card reads the same ring
from the island it already holds, and two laws follow from it:
- **a weld split on the ring** (a ring point whose kept copies hold two floats) is a certain refusal: the carve's
  exact-weld law ("two float positions"), or, where the lift rounds the copies apart, its ring accounting ("not
  degree-2"). It caught all 28 such refusals in the sample, and two geometry ones, and no pass. (Refusing on any
  split key on the ring, the looser form, would have refused one pass.) The live island has 122 split keys, mostly
  round an older carve's foot in blocks (6-7, 18-19) and in (22-23, 5-7), all from before this arc: the C10 forest
  at (1534, -456) added none (its block before and after: the same 80 in its window);
- **the zip's edge bound**: every ring point is joined to some rim point, so the farthest ring point's distance to
  the rim bounds the zip's longest edge from below; past ``MTN_MAX_EDGE`` + 0.5 (the ghost is not de-tilted, so its
  rim may drift a little from the carve's) the carve's geometry gate refuses. It caught 4 in the sample.

The keys are the carve's own: ``kk3`` of Python floats (its positions are Python floats; numpy's rounding is not
Python's), memoised per vertex on the island index, so a redraw costs 13 ms median (11 without the laws; 33 before
the memo).

## What the preview cannot catch, and why

- **Down faces in the zip and the plugs** (34 of the 46 geometry refusals): the ghost's own rock never faces down
  (0 of 95 placements); the faces are the zip's, built from the lifted ground and the rim by the carve's dynamic
  programme. Predicting them is running the carve again.
- **The zip's rise and winding** (12-16): they depend on the lifted ring's heights, the apron's field, which the
  card does not compute.
- **Annulus cracks** (15 of the geometry ones): once-edges the carve mints; the same.

These stay with the dry run, and each is marked.
