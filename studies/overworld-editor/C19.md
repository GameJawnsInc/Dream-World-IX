# C19: edit (or delete) a step that is already in the game

**Status:** BUILT, 2026-10-24 (`c19_lab.py`, real bytes on the live install). The roadmap's "editing a promoted step
in place: today you have to revert the promote first; the live folder's ledger has to stay true" ("Keep going with
the next roadmap item").

## The design: three proven operations, chained

Editing a step that is in the game cannot mean writing the live folder's bytes behind the draft's back: the draft's
history and its ledger copy stop at the promoted fence (a promote rebases the draft on the live folder and takes its
ledger), so undoing below the fence would let the draft's ledger drift from the live one -- the one thing that must
stay true. So the bar chains what already keeps it true by construction:

1. **out of the game**: `world-promote --revert` for each promote that stands over the step, newest first, until it
   is no longer promoted. Revert already refuses over later work at its blocks (another session's), and restores the
   rest exactly from its snapshot;
2. **the edit**: C9's, unchanged -- the card opens on the step's row, the steps after it go back on top;
3. **back into the game**: `world-promote` once the edit closes -- Accept, Cancel, or a step that refuses on the new
   ground -- so the game holds the edited recipe, or exactly what it held.

Delete takes the same path. The bar asks first, since the game loses the promote while the edit is open. **A revert
refusing after another went through** promotes straight back, so the game is never left half reverted; a cancel
promotes again only from a draft known to be as it was.

## The acceptance

**On real bytes** (`c19_lab.py build`: the real RecipeBar and hill card, real runners, the live FF9CustomMap-world; a
draft copy of it first, as the snapshot): a hill at (64, -512) accepted and promoted from its card; the bar picks it
(in the game), Edit reverts, opens the card on its own row, the hill moves to (64, -504), Accept; the bar promotes
again. Jobs: `world-promote --revert`, the hill's own checks and Accept, `world-promote`; 35.5 s.
- **While the edit was open the game's folder was exactly the snapshot** (discs 1 and 4), and the card said the step's
  row back.
- **I1: the live disc 1 == the edited recipe composed onto the snapshot, over 999 world files.**
- **L1, THE LEDGER STAYS TRUE: every one of the 72 live files at the hill's changed blocks has a last ledger row
  naming its own bytes** (40 written by `world-hill`).
- **R1: a final revert puts the live folder back exactly as the snapshot found it**: the promote stack holds, and the
  lab leaves nothing behind.

No game run: the bytes are promote's and revert's own, walked in game since C4.

**Hermetic** (`tests/test_worldedit.py`): an in-game step edited (out of the game while open, the live folder at the
end == the edited recipe composed onto the folder as it was), cancelled (the game exactly as it was), deleted (the
game without it), a revert that refuses (nothing opens; the game and the draft stay), and two promotes over the step
with the older's revert refusing (promoted straight back: the game exactly as it was). Each recovery path, broken,
turns its test red.
