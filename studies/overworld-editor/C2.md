# C2: the mountain in the editor

**Status:** built 2026-10-23; in game every registered prediction held (`c2_lab.py run`); **owner-passed: "walked it without issue"**. The massif stays promoted in FF9CustomMap-world (the owner keeps world-map edits). Owner request: "C2".

## What it does

"Add mountain" sits beside "Add forest" on the 3D view's bar. Its card is the forest's (C1) with the massif's
handles:

- **Donor:** the seven qualified massifs of `donors.toml` (`world-donors --class massif`), nothing else.
- **Angle** 0-359 (`--rot`), **spread** 1.0-×`MTN_SPREAD_MAX` 1.5, **rock height** 1.0-×`MTN_ROCK_HEIGHT_MAX` 2.0,
  each stopping at the interior's own constant. The rock-height handle holds at ×1 for a massif `--rock-height`
  refuses (the horseshoe: falls and a river mouth on its face).
- **The home-ground chip:** the ground the massif stands on at home (`home_ground`) beside the ground under the
  ghost; it warns when they differ. THE HOME-GROUND WARNING is not a refusal (the carry is lawful and no gate sees
  it), so a warned verdict still offers Accept, and its banner says why.
- **The ghost** is the carve's own shape: the de-tilted blob seated on the island's ground, turned and spread by the
  carve's map, and stretched by `interior._rock_height_map` itself (tens of milliseconds, cached per height).
- **The command** is `world-mountain --center ... --donor <rect>` with the handles that are not defaults, and
  `--ground <the island's family>` and `--reach` only when needed (a placed massif wider than the default 96u read
  window, the snow ridge spread past ×1.2).
- Show in game after a Promote goes to the carve's own teleport point, beside the massif (its centre is rock).

Both tools write the one draft per folder, and a card that opens re-reads the island (the other tool may have
written it). One card is open at a time.

## The preview is the law

`world.mountainedit.footprint_preview` is `carve_mountain`'s exact-placement laws in their order, vectorised:

1. **blocks:** the span the massif needs (the single-block pipeline, the continent route beside deployed neighbours,
   or the multi-block core) holds deployed islands, in the read window the CLI loads;
2. **span:** the placed rim stays `band + 2` inside the span;
3. **footprint:** no non-plain tri of the island's ground family in or within the 6.5u band of the rim (the carve's
   `band_offenders`, now one function that says which tris).

Plus the peak cap at the ghost's own seat when the rock is stretched. On the real island (FF9CustomMap-world, 60
random spots, donors, angles and spreads) the preview named the carve's first placement gate 60/60, at about 4 ms.
The hermetic fence (`test_footprint_preview_is_the_carve_s_own_law`, a single-block pyramid and a two-block cone
over a patchy field) goes red when the band shrinks or the corner test is dropped.

## B1 for the mountain

`carve_mountain`'s refusals are `CarveRefused` with a gate and world points; the messages are unchanged:
`blocks` (block middles), `span` (the rim points past the edge), `footprint` (the offending tris, and the detail
names what they are), `hole`, `leak`, `geometry` (the crack edges and the longest edge), `zip` (the steepest zip
tri), `rigid`, `peak`, `apron` (the steepest apron tri), `probe` (the probe point). `world-mountain --report-json`
writes the verdict as the forest's does, plus the ground and the home-ground note.

## The window

The massif's sizes go on a second row: one row of seven controls put the window's floor at 1218px at 100%. With
two rows the card is no wider than the forest's (925/952px at 100%, 1139/1152px at 150%) and 39px taller.
Then fixed on its own branch (`fix/carry-card-floors`): the promoted forest card's one row of seven controls set a
1348px floor at 150%, and at 150% in a 1280x850 window both cards were squeezed (the window's minimum height was 931
for the forest, 984 for the mountain), clipping the verdict and the draft line. Now Accept, Copy command and Discard
draft share a row, and the draft line takes the next row with Promote, Revert promote and Show in game beside it; while
a card is open the block strip below gives way, the 3D view's floor drops from 180 to 32px (`CARD_VIEW_FLOOR`) and a
plain caption folds (the draw box names the stack, the card's draft line the draft; a caption carrying a warning never
folds); and the view's caption has a line of its own under the bar's controls (beside them it wrapped to four lines at
150%, five once the draft is drawn). The fold and the 32px floor are the room C3's recipe bar needs above the cards:
its caption wraps to three lines at 150% (72px, not the ~40 planned), and with it every 150% state still shows its whole
verdict, the view a 32px strip in the tallest (the mountain's three-line pass). Native gui_snap, mist, 1280x850, window minimumSizeHint:
150%: every card state 1139/1152 wide (promoted forest was 1348); 100%: 925/952. Every 150% state, the mountain's new
`-accepted` and `-promoted` among them, shows its whole verdict and draft line. The minimum HEIGHT the snap prints no
longer tells clipping (737-814 at 150%): it leaves out word-wrap, so read the PNGs.
