# C21: the gold chocobo's camera in game -- and how any vehicle was reached

**Status:** MEASURED + DECODE CORRECTED, 2026-10-24 (`c21_lab.py`, three game runs). The roadmap's last item, "the
gold chocobo's camera in game" (C8 left it decoded but not measured, with no boarding route).

**The answer.** C8 gave the flying gold chocobo the airship's final Y nudges, 1419 / 996. That holds on disc 4
only. On disc 1 it flies with the walker's 558 / 312, and a Dead Pepper fed in the air raises it to 1419 / 996. The
same mechanism also corrected two vehicles C8 had only met loaded aboard: on disc 1 the Hilda Garde III and the
Blue Narciss boarded on foot take 558 / 312. All eight predictions were registered before their run, and every one
held.

## The mechanism (the source)

FixTypeCam's nudges ease toward a TARGET, 25 raw a frame (`ff9.w_cameraUpdate`). Only three things write that
target:
- **`w_movementChange`** (RunWorldCode 1), by type_cam: 0 and 1 give 558 / 312, 2 gives 320 / 701, 3 gives 1419 / 996. For
  **type_cam 4, the gold chocobo flying, it sets the nudge but not the target**, so the next frame eases it back to
  the ground chocobo's 558 / 312.
- **RunScriptSync with tag 19** sets 1419 / 996, and **tag 21** sets 558 / 312 (`EventEngine.DoEventCode`, REQEW,
  world mode). The hook keys on the tag NUMBER alone, whatever function runs under it.

A census of each free-roam dispatcher's own request blocks (`gamecam.DISPATCHER_TAGS`, re-derived from the install
by `test_the_tag_table_is_the_install_s_own`):

| dispatcher | tag 19 | tag 21 |
|---|---|---|
| 9000, 9005 | the Dead Pepper's rise in the air | an object's script (no mode change) |
| 9003 | the Dead Pepper's rise | **the Blue Narciss boarded on foot** (after RunWorldCode(1, 7)) |
| 9007 | the Dead Pepper's rise | **the Hilda Garde III boarded on foot** (after RunWorldCode(1, 8)) |
| 9008, 9009 (disc 4) | **the take-off** ([190] = 6) | the landing (then RunWorldCode(1, 5)) |
| 9002, 9010, 9011 | -- | the tent (on foot: no change) |

WORLD08 / 09 number the chocobo's functions one higher than WORLD00-07: their Dead Pepper is tag 20, which no hook
reads. The hook fits disc 4's numbering, where tag 19 is the take-off, and misfires on disc 1's.

## The route

C8's boardings (`veh_lib.reach_world_as`: field 6603 at a ScenarioCounter, the bytes poked, out of the door).
- **The gold chocobo** boards on the ground: mode 5, [191] 5, flags 809 / 810. It is teleported to C8's forest
  spot (topograph 37) and takes off with Confirm; take-off and landing are forest-only (topographs 36-38 in entry
  2's switch).
- **The Dead Pepper** comes from `c21_pepper.field.toml`, a scratch room at 31300 that the new game passes through
  once. Deployed per run and reverted after it.
- **The pepper menu** (Triangle) needs Global.Byte[187] bit 0 (poked). It read "Feed him a Dead Pepper (Remaining:
  3)", and the rise ran: 18.04 up to 49.2 and back. The script predicts 31.25u; it measured 31.2.
- **The airships** were parked at C8's LOW record, then walked into and boarded with Confirm.
- **The Blue Narciss** was parked 2u off a Beach1 shore (`prep2`) and boarded the same way. Run 2's attempt ended
  in a random battle, and run 3 (encounters off, a looser facing tolerance on the slow sand) boarded it.

## The measure, checked first

Each still is scored against the decode drawn twice, once per nudge pair, with everything else equal. The measures
are C8's edge map and colour classes, with the renders fogged as C18 measured for the still's year (no mist at SC
9500 / 10650, the Mist on disc 4).

**Calibrated on C8's ten stills, whose nudges the source fixes** (`calibrate`): edges picked the right pair 9 of 10
times, with margins of 0.1-0.4. The one miss, air-high, is a walk / risen tie at 0.01. Colour classes scored 7 of
10. A margin of at least 0.1 on edges counts as decisive.

In flight the edge margins fell to 0.04-0.07, under that line, so a second witness was added that needs no
terrain: **the party's own screen row**. The aim nudge moves the party in the frame. The decode projects the
party's position, and the game draws the gold chocobo's feet (saturated yellow). On the ground (C8's confirmed
chocobo camera), the feet sit 30.5 rows above the projected point, which is the model's own offset. Each still in
flight is judged by which pair leaves that same offset.

## What changed

- **`world/gamecam.py`**:
  - New: `fix_target(mode, how=, world= | disc=)`, `dispatcher_for`, `DISPATCHER_TAGS`, `FIX_ON_CHANGE`, `TAG_FIX`.
  - `how` is `boarded` (the default: as play reaches it), `pepper`, or `loaded` (the dispatcher loaded aboard:
    back from the airship's bridge, a battle's return, a save).
  - `pose(how=, world=, disc=, fix=)` replaces `airborne`; the pose reports its `how` and `fix`.
- **"As the game sees it"**: the vehicle chooser's items carry how the vehicle was reached. There are two new items,
  "Gold chocobo, after a Dead Pepper" and "Hilda Garde III, back from its bridge". The view draws disc 1's
  dispatchers.
- `c7_compare.py` / `c8_fit.py` pass `how="loaded"`: C7's and C8's routes load the dispatcher aboard, so their
  numbers stand.
- Tests:
  - every measured state's target;
  - the table re-derived from the install's dispatchers; a deleted row or a dropped hook turns both red;
  - the chooser's items and the lift they draw.

## Not here

- **The flying gold chocobo LOADED** (a dispatcher load with [190] = 6) was not measured. The tweaker's target at
  load is whatever it held. `fix_target` gives the walker's 558 / 312, and no ordinary play path was found that
  loads mid-flight.
- **The disc-4 Blue Narciss** (9009's own boat arm, no tag 21: 320 / 701 by the census) was not walked.
- **A world-aware 3D view.** It draws disc 1, so a disc-4 camera needs `fix_target(disc=4)` from code.
