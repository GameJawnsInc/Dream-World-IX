cards across the x seam." C10 did this for the relief cards and left these four ("a carry's rect does not wrap:
`transplant_region`'s own bound").

So the work was the carry. The sink and the coast morph stock blocks only, and no stock block crosses the seam. A
kit landmass that does (the west-seam continent) is undone through its own record, not sunk.

## What changed

**`transplant_region`** (the multi-block carry) takes a target rect that runs past column 23: the anchor must be on
the grid, and the rect may wrap. Rows stay edged. Inside, every target key stays UNWRAPPED, since column 24 is
column 0's east neighbour, and the Wang, texture and orphan gates judge the region's own adjacency. A column takes
its wrapped label only where it meets a file:
- the stock occupancy read (a real block at the wrapped column refuses, naming it);
- the donor-identity compare;
- the mod-overwrite gate;
- the effective-prefab gate's name;
- the deploy: meshes (`_seam_label`, `interior._wrap_label`'s relabel) and `Donor.txt` sidecars;
- the report's `cells`, and `--report-json`'s `blocks`, which the editor's undo snapshots by.

A block's verts are block-local (24 × 64 = the world's width), so the relabel moves no byte. The orphan census already
read its ring at the labels (C14).

**The carry card** (`transplantedit`): `anchor_for` wraps the rect's column instead of clamping it, so a click just
east or just west of x = 0 anchors at column 23. `cells` names labels for a rect too, so the preview's occupancy and
real-land laws ask the right blocks. The ghost keeps unwrapped world coordinates (x 1472-1600), and the view draws
every overlay's twin across the seam (`seam_shifts`), so it shows under a click at either end.
