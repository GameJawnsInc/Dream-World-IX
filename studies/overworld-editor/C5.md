# C5: the other verbs -- sinking an island, shaping a coast

**Status:** two verbs built and in game 2026-10-23: the sink (`c5_lab.py`: the island went) and coast morphs
(`c5c_lab.py`: the cliff came out, 4/4 predictions). River placement is deferred with a measured reason (below); island
and transplant are the rest of the rung, not done here. Owner requests: "go on with C5", then "go".

## What it does

"Sink an island" sits beside Add forest and Add mountain. Its card is the carry cards' frame with a sink's parts:

- **The islands are the cached island list** (`world.sinkedit.candidates`): `transplant.sink_candidates`, every real
  island of the disc with its verdict, now with its **outline** (`_plan_hull`, the convex hull of its land). It reads
  every stock block, 3 min 13 s on disc 1, so it is cached per install and disc in the kit's cache: stock land does
  not move under a draft (the sink reads stock).
- **A click picks the island under it** (the smallest outline holding the point, else one within 6u), and the
  command uses the island's own point (the one `world-sink --list` prints), not the click.
- **The ghost is its outline at sea level**, filled: where the sea closes.
- **The preview is the island's precomputed verdict**: a whole-tile sink, its land only with its neighbours kept, or
  the cluster its water joins ("With its cluster"); an island with an entrance needs "Allow entrances".
- **Check** runs `world-sink --dry-run --report-json` (new: the verdict as data, the failing gates' blocks as
  marks). **Accept** runs `world-sink` into the draft twice: disc 1, then the same sink on disc 4 with
  `--skip-mirror`.
- Each Accept is a `[[sink]]` recipe row. Show in game is not offered for a sink: the island's point is open sea.

## THE DRAFT'S DISC-4 HALF

`world-sink`'s disc-4 step is the mirror, which copies the edit where disc 4's cell matches disc 1 and REPLAYS the sink
on disc 4's own ground where it differs (the whole edit, atomically). A draft cannot mirror: it has no bundles, so the
mirror logs NOT RUN there and the replay never fires. So the draft runs the replay's own call itself (disc 4,
`--skip-mirror`). On promote, the live mirror copies over it where the discs match and holds it where they differ,
so disc 4 ends as `world-sink` itself would leave it. A `[[sink]]` row composed INTO A DRAFT makes the same second
call (`sinkedit.is_draft`), so an opened recipe writes the draft the tab wrote. In game here, block (4,8) differs on
disc 4: the mirror held the whole edit ("NOT MIRRORED ... Disc 4 keeps its own ground there") and the promoted
disc-4 files are the draft's own disc-4 sink.

## Found on the way: promote failed onto cells the live folder had nothing at

The first `run`'s promote wrote the live folder and the mirror pass, and then failed to write its record: the
record folder was made only by the snapshot, and a sink of a stock island has nothing to snapshot (no live file at
those cells). The live folder held the sink with no record to revert it by, and the draft was not rebased.
**Fixed** (`draft.promote` makes the record folder first; `test_promote_onto_cells_the_live_folder_has_nothing_at`
fails without it), and **recovered** by `evidence/c5/c5_recover.py`, which rebuilt the record exactly from the state
the promote left (every edited file the draft's bytes in the live folder, nothing else at those cells, the history
mark already set) and rebased the draft. The checks after the promote then ran on the still-open game
(`c5_lab.py resume`).

Records: `evidence/c5/c5_prep.json`, `evidence/c5/c5_result.json`, `evidence/c5/c5_recover.py`.

## Shaping a coast (`world-transplant --in-place` at a lawful window)

"Shape a coast" is the carry cards' frame with a coast morph's parts:

- **The windows are `world-morphs`' catalog** (`world.coastedit.windows` over `coastscan.scan_block`: a block's beach
  waterline runs and cliff base runs, each verb probed down its depth ladder by the real builders and certified by an
  in-place dry run). About 6 s per block, cached per install, disc and block.
- **A click picks the block and the lawful window nearest it**; the verb picker offers only that window's lawful
  morphs, and the depth spin stops at the window's own ceiling (a beach verb runs from its landward ceiling,
  negative, to its seaward one). The window's run is the ghost.
- **A block the draft or a folder of the stack already overrides offers no window** (`coastedit.edited`): the windows
  are stock's, and an in-place morph would read that folder's bytes instead.
- Check runs `world-transplant --in-place --<verb> "x0,z0:x1,z1:D" --dry-run --report-json` (new: the morph's
  verdict as data). Accept runs the morph into the draft on disc 1 and then on disc 4: the in-place morph's disc-4
  step is a replay too, so THE DRAFT'S DISC-4 HALF applies (the shared card now runs a verb's extra calls,
  `CarryTool._accept_calls`).
- A `[[morph]]` table in `world-fuse` (cell, verb, window, depth, allow_entrances; the base tier) runs
  `world-transplant --in-place` itself through the CLI's own parser, with the draft's disc-4 call when it composes
  into a draft. Each Accept is a `[[morph]]` recipe row.

**On real bytes** (`tests/test_worldcoast.py`, install-gated): block (2,7)'s cliff bump at 2.5, the disc-4-replay
study's passing case. The card's two calls, the same two typed by hand, and the recipe's `[[morph]]` row composed
into a draft write the same bytes, and the draft edits disc 4 too.

**On the real island** (`c5c_lab.py build`): a click at (177, -480) picked block (2,7)'s 116 u cliff run; the only
lawful verb was cliff-bump, to 2.5 u. Check 13.2 s, Accept 11.6 s (two jobs). **I1: identical over 1,545 world
files**; the draft edited Terrain and Sea4 at (2,7) on both discs.

## River placement: deferred, measured

`world-river` carries the stock river cluster onto an island it mints round it on open ocean, placed by a whole-block
shift (`--at DX,DZ`). The island takes 12 blocks; on disc 1 exactly 56 open-ocean shifts fit it, and **every one of
the 56 is already overridden in FF9CustomMap-world** (its islands, and the river the rung-4 carry placed at 512,128;
8 of them in the C1 lab too). So a river tool on this folder could only show red everywhere, or re-place the
existing river where it is. It waits for a folder with open ocean to spare, or for a "move the river" that is
`world-river --remove` then a placement.
