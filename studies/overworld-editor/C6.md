# C6: the hill -- the last relief verb in the World tab

**Status:** BUILT + IN GAME, 2026-10-23 (`c6h_lab.py`: the party stood on the hill's top at the predicted height).
Owner request: "go on with what's next in the editor plan". The plan's old Rung 6 named the hill among the verbs

declared its handles.

## What it does

"Add a hill" (in "Edit the map", after Add mountain) is the carry cards' frame (`workspace/worldhill.py` over
`world/hilledit.py`):

- **A click places the centre**, a drag moves it, and two spins set the height and the radius. They stop where the
  handle table says (`world/handles.py`: height 2.0-6.0, radius 12-30). Those are the editor's stops, wider than the
  language on both sides, because the verb has no range ceiling of its own: the flank law and the peak cap judge each
  pair.
- **The ghost IS the hill the verb writes.** `interior.build_hill` is a pure-Y displacement of the island's deployed
  vertices by `raised_cosine(distance, height, radius)`. `hilledit.lifted` applies that very formula to the same
  vertices of the island soup the card already reads. A test pins the ghost to `build_hill`'s output, triangle for
  triangle.
- **The preview runs the verb's own laws live**, over the same triangles in its order:
  - the footprint: pure mains in a disc of radius + 2, at least 60 triangles, relief under 2.4u, and clear of forest
    (12u), stamps (4u) and rock (6u);
  - every displaced triangle mains;
  - the flank: no face over `MAX_FLANK` (28.6°), none facing down;
  - the peak cap (`PEAK_CAP` 8.6).
  `world-hill --dry-run` runs the rest on Check: the crack gate and the placement census.
- Accept raises it in the draft. The recipe row is `[[hill]]` (`center`, and `height`/`radius` off their defaults).

**THE PREVIEW AGREES WITH THE VERB, GATE FOR GATE** (`tests/test_worldhill.py`, seven cases spanning passes, flank and
footprint refusals). Where the verb refuses with a gate the preview leaves to Check, the preview passes. The case in
the set is a hill reaching the island's edge: every cheap law is green, and the crack gate refuses on Check, because
the lift tears the edge.

## Underneath

- **B1 reaches the hill: its refusals say where.** `build_hill` raised plain strings. It now raises
  `interior.CarveRefused` per gate: footprint at the centre (or the scan point), flank at the steepest or down-facing
  triangles, peak at the top vertex, crack at the torn edges. The messages are unchanged, so the CLI text is
  byte-identical.
- `world-hill --report-json` (the carry verbs' verdict shape: `_carry_report_json`).
- B2's table: the hill's centre, height and radius are on the gui surface now, and the fence learned two things. It
  checks every card that reads a table (forest, mountain, hill), and **a table with gui handles must have a card**:
  otherwise those handles exist in the table only.
- `recipe.label` takes the donor catalogue's class from the handle table (B2's spirit: it named "canopy"/"massif"
  by hand).
