# C8: the game's own camera on a vehicle -- the boat, the chocobo, the airship

**Status:** BUILT + MEASURED, 2026-10-23. Owner request: "Go on with the game camera for vehicles (the boat, the
chocobo, the airship) in 'As the game sees it'". C7 decoded the chase camera on foot; this extends it.

**The answer.**
- **The Blue Narciss: confirmed.** Its own decode scores a land IoU of 0.90, against 0.05 for the on-foot camera,
  and both stills' fits stay at the decode.
- **A chocobo: confirmed**, including the forest rule. Colour-class agreement is 0.97-0.98 and the fits stay at the
  decode (land and edge measures cannot tell it from on foot: the frames are all land).
- **The airship (Hilda Garde III): consistent, not tightly pinned.** Every fit scatters around the decode within the
  same spread the validated on-foot stills show, with no consistent direction. Its frames are all land and WORLD07's
  dusk tints them orange, which weakens every measure.
- **The flying gold chocobo: decoded, not measured** (no proven boarding route).

## The decode (`world/gamecam.py`)

Each travel MODE (`gEventGlobal[190]`) names a movement `type` and a `type_cam` in `TransportControls.csv`. Memoria
reads that file through the mod stack, first folder wins, and `gamecam.transport_table` does the same. The live
folder's copy matches stock on every vehicle row; its probe row 10 differs, which proves the stack read.

| mode | vehicle | type | type_cam | the camera, beyond C7's element row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | on foot | 0 | 0 | (C7) |
| 1-5 | the ground chocobos | 0 | 1 | over forest (topographs 36-38) the view takes FieldOfView itself |
| 6 | the gold chocobo, flying | 1 | 4 | flight's view and floor; its take-off script (RunScriptSync tag 19) sets the airship's nudges, landing (tag 21) puts the walker's back |
| 7 | the Blue Narciss | 2 | 2 | pers / 9.5 (not 8); the final nudges 320 / 701 |
| 8, 9 | the Hilda Garde III, the Invincible | 1 | 3 | the view = FieldOfView itself; the eye never below 17.578125 and its terrain probe capped at 33.2; the nudges 1419 / 996 |

The final Y nudges come from `FixTypeCam`, which is on by default (`WorldState`'s constructor), scaled by CameraHeight
and CameraAimHeight. At rest nothing else applies: the speed's field-of-view widening needs the craft moving, and the
turn's roll eases back to zero.

## The measurement (`c8_lab.py`, `c8_fit.py`)

**Boarding.** The vehicle study's DLL-free routes board each vehicle for real: field 6603 at the ScenarioCounter whose
exit cascade loads the wanted dispatcher, `[190]` (+`[191]`) and the vehicle's record poked, then out of the door.
The world script binds control to the vehicle actor at load, so the altitudes are real.
- **The Hilda Garde III** on WORLD07: stills at its spawn (y 2.78), climbed (27.6, and the same turned 28°), over the
  high ground (28.86: the floor raise) and at the ceiling over the summit (exactly 42.1875).
- **A yellow chocobo** on WORLD07: lawn (4.42, two yaws) and forest (8.66).
- **The Blue Narciss** on WORLD03, at -0.703125: water level less 0.703, as the vehicle study measured.
- **The boat's spawn is new.** The vehicle study's lane at block (21,1) is now the comma C5 carried there. `c8_lab.py
  prep` finds the nearest point the live stack reads as deep sea with a coast 24-60u away, (257.76, -381.15), and
  encodes its record: x and z x256 as 24-bit, y x256 as 16-bit, then the facing. The encoder reproduces the vehicle
  study's airship record exactly.

**Three measures, and why three.** The land mask (C7's) saturates on all-land frames: it scored a 7-degree view a
perfect fit on the airship. The colour classes (water, grass, sand, rock) fail on WORLD07's dusk, which tints the rock
orange. The edge map (the luminance gradient, blurred and correlated) ignores colour and light. Each measure was first
run on C7's validated on-foot stills to know its noise.

The edge fit's spread on the validated on-foot stills is about ±3u in eye height, ±3 degrees in yaw and field of view,
and ±10% in distance. The airship's fits sit inside that spread, scattered in sign (distance 0.95-1.1, eye -1.75 to
+1). So nothing shows a bias in the flight decode, and nothing pins it tighter than the measure allows. The numbers
(no frames) are in `evidence/c8/`.

## What changed

- **`world/gamecam.py`**: `transport_table` (the stack's `TransportControls.csv`), `pose(mode=, table=, airborne=,
  topograph=)`, the flight / boat / forest rules above.
- **"As the game sees it"**: with the game camera on, the bar shows a **vehicle chooser** (on foot, chocobo, gold
  chocobo flying, Blue Narciss, Hilda Garde III, Invincible) and, for a flyer, a **height** spin. The party sits as
  each vehicle sits, as measured: on foot or a chocobo on the ground it stands on (over forest, the canopy's stand
  height), the boat at the water's surface less 0.703, a flyer at the height asked, raised to the ground below and
  capped at 42.1875. The chooser shows about 12 characters so it never sets the window's floor (952 / 1154px).
- `c7_compare.py` grew the colour classes and the edge measure, and a control score with the on-foot camera.

## Not here

The flying gold chocobo in game (no proven boarding route: the decode is the source's alone). The camera while the
craft moves (the speed's widening and the turn's roll). A tint-proof measure that pins flight to the settle bands, as
the land mask pinned on foot.
