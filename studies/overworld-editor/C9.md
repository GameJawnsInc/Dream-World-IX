# C9: editing a placed shape -- pick it, move it, retune it or delete it

**Status:** BUILT + IN GAME, 2026-10-24 (`c9_lab.py`: a hill picked, moved and lowered; in game it stood where the
edit put it, and its old place was lawn again). Owner request: "Go on with editing placed shapes: pick a placed shape in the 3D view,
move, turn, retune or delete it, and re-compose the recipe from that row." C3 left it in its Not here ("editing a row
in the middle of the recipe"); until now a shape could only be undone with everything placed after it.

## What it does

- **A click picks a placed step.** With no card open, a click on the 3D view picks the recipe step placed there
  (`recipe.step_at`): of the steps that changed the clicked block, the one placed nearest the click (the later one on
  a tie, as it lies on top). A sunk island is picked by clicking the sea where it was. A pin marks the step's place,
  and the recipe bar names it: "Step 1 of 3: forest forest-blob at 26, -26." with **Edit** and **Delete** beside Undo.
- **Edit opens the step's own card on its own handles.** Each card that can say a row (`load_row`: the forest, the
  mountain, the hill, the island, the carry) sets its handles from the step's row and places its ghost there; a
  test pins `load_row` as the inverse of `row()`, so an edit that changes nothing writes the same row. The card says
  "Edit forest", "Accept the edit" and "Cancel edit"; Promote, Revert and Discard wait.
  - The card judges the shape against the draft **as it was before the step**: Edit undoes back to it, byte for byte,
    and lifts the step and the ones after it out of the history (`draft.begin_edit`, the records parked under
    `history/edit/`). So the shape's own old footprint is free, and the dry run is the one the recipe will compose.
  - Drag it, turn it, retune it; the cheap laws and Check work as for a new shape. Accept writes the edited shape in
    the step's place.
- **Delete** puts nothing in its place (after a confirm that says how many steps come back on top).
- **Then the steps after it go back on top, in their order.** Each is **replayed** from its record (`draft.replay`:
  its after-images, its own ledger rows appended; no verb runs) where none of the blocks it read or wrote changed,
  and otherwise **composed again** by `world-fuse`, one job per step, as Open recipe does. THE DIRTY SET
  (`draft.dirty_after`) carries which blocks may differ from the draft as it was: a block both versions of a step
  wrote alike is clean again, any other block either wrote is dirty. A step's reach is its row's read window
  (`recipe.replay_window`: `row_cells`, the window the history snapshots) plus the cells it changed; a replay also
  refuses by itself when the step's files are not as it found them.
- **A step that refuses on the new ground stops the edit**, and the draft goes back exactly as it was
  (`draft.cancel_edit`: the put-back steps undone, the lifted records restored and redone); the bar says which step
  refused and why. Cancel edit (the card's, or the bar's while steps are going back) and closing the card do the same.
- **The edited steps are ordinary steps**: Undo walks them, Save writes the edited recipe.
- **What cannot be edited:** a step already in the game (promoted; Revert promote first, as for Undo), and a sink, a
  coast morph or a river move: no card opens on those rows, so their Edit is off and says why; Delete works.

## The acceptance

**Hermetic** (`tests/test_worldedit.py`):
- three steps placed through the World tab (a forest, a mountain beside it, a forest far off); a click picks the
  first, Edit opens its card on the same row, the forest moves; the mountain is composed again (its window holds the
  forest's block), the far forest replayed; **the draft is byte for byte `world-fuse` of the edited recipe composed
  onto the folder**. The same for a delete. A refusing step leaves the draft and its history exactly as they were.
  Closing the card cancels; a promoted step's Edit and Delete are off.
- the kit half: cancel restores bytes, ledger and history (undone steps still redoable); replay refuses when its
  files or its ledger moved; the dirty set; promote refuses an open edit; an undo that refuses on the way down leaves
  the draft as it was; the pick.
- **Broken on purpose** (each red, then restored): replay never used (2 tests red); the dirty set always empty (4);
  cancel without the redo (4). Found: on this bench, replaying the mountain instead of running it again writes the
  same bytes (its carve does not reach the forest's block), so only the dirty-set decision itself is fenced there,
  not its consequence. The real-bytes lab below measures the consequence.

**On real bytes** (`c9_lab.py build`: the real RecipeBar and HillTool, real runners, a draft of FF9CustomMap-world,
wired as the World tab wires them):
- three hills through the card, nearest the Landing Site: H1 at (64, -512), H2 at (40, -472) (its read window
  holds H1's blocks), H3 at (144, -1056) (its window holds none of them). Checks 8-10 s, Accepts 8-14 s.

**Found by the first run: a promote that crashed half way.** The first `run` stopped at N2: the edited draft's
promote raised `KeyError` in its relabel pass after it had written four files, before the disc-4 mirror and before
its promote record (so Revert promote could not undo it). The relabel (C5's river move) re-ledgers a file whose
bytes a draft left alone but whose ledger names another writer, and it took "the draft's last row differs from the
live folder's" as "the draft wrote this row". Here the live ledger had moved on after the edited draft was copied
(ORIG promoted and reverted there), so the rows differed though the draft wrote nothing, and the inherited row for
block (1,8) was an old one naming no writer: a relabel with no tag. **THE FIX:** a draft relabels only rows it wrote
itself (a row the live ledger does not hold) that name a writer, and every tag is known before the first byte moves.
`test_a_second_draft_promotes_after_another_draft_of_the_folder_promoted_and_reverted` is the crash, hermetic (red on
the old promote, the same KeyError). The live folder was put back from ORIG's promote snapshot (`c9_repair.py`: the
four files restored through the deploy seam, the relabelled one ledgered again, all 108 files at the six blocks
checked against the snapshot; the ledger backed up first), and the second `run` passed. The crashed run's record:
`evidence/c9/c9_result.crashed-promote2.json`.

## Not here

Editing a promoted step in place (Revert promote first). A card for the sink, the coast morph and the river move. A
step whose read window is the whole map (an island, a carry, a sink) is always composed again after an edit before
it, never replayed: their `row_cells` are the whole world. (C11 gave those three a measured window; the coast
morph and the river move still always run again.)
