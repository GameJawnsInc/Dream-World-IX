## The owner's design ruling this session

**Landability policy = `cliffs-refuse`.** Water fronting high walls gets topo **54** — boat-legal, so
you can sail up to it, but the get-off gate demands **53**, so you cannot disembark. Consequence,
accepted deliberately: **the Rung-5a island is unlandable by boat.** That is the coherent form of
"islands without beaches exist" — such an island is not a boat destination, rather than one we bolt a
beach onto. Any island intended as a destination needs a genuine low shore.

Deployed classes on Disc9 (`cliffs-refuse`): KEEL 468 · BELT 282 · CLIFF 1128 · BEACH **0**.
Under `land-anywhere` the same 1128 verts would be BEACH; seal and belt are identical either way.
