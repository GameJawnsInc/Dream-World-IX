## The instrument

THE CUT-LINE LAW (`../../overworld-topography/README.md`, the v4 transplant census): a lawful cut crosses only land
at y <= 6.5, and high relief is cut around, never through. A massif carry's rim sits on lowland, so the smallest
lawful unit holding the falls is the whole high component it stands in. `falls_recon.py`:
- **A. The system:** cluster D's water parts welded into one component; its tips (plateau end, valley end), each
  with its nearest rock and sea.
- **B. The high components, map-wide** (terrain tris with top > 6.5, joined by shared plan edges, as 5c's
  `high_component`), and the one each seed lands in:
  - cluster D's falls lip, its plateau tip and its valley tip;
  - cluster C's source tip (5c's seed);
  - **the control:** the horseshoe's falls lip. It is a carriable falls, so its component must come out bounded,
    about the catalogued massif (blocks 5-6,15-16). If it floods the continent, the instrument cannot tell a
    carriable falls from an uncarriable one and B decides nothing.
- **C. The rock component** (topo 49 joined by shared edges, the massif census's definition) holding the rock the
  falls welds to, ranked map-wide. The massif census calls its largest one, comp0, "the continental rock network"
  and does not qualify it.
- **D. The falls itself, D against the horseshoe:** sheets, drop, and what every falls boundary edge welds to (top,
  bottom, sides).

  - **The horseshoe:** its tops weld to its Object (topo 59: 3 edges on each sheet), the bottom to the 58 shelf and

  This matches the catalog note that the horseshoe's ring is owned by Object, Falls, River and RiverJoint.
- **THE SPRAY IS PINNED TO HOME.** The engine's `AlexandriaWaterfall` world effect is data, not a mesh: 6 emitters
  (SPS 20, rnd 512) in `discmr.img`'s pack, sub-table 45 (41 + WorldEffect 4, `ff9.cs:8863-8865`).

  - `UseWorldEffect` returns true for it unconditionally (`WorldConfiguration.cs:209`).
  - So a carried copy of this falls would land without its spray, and the spray would keep playing at home.
  - The horseshoe has no such effect, which is part of why its carry is honest.
- **One river, two stretches.** Cluster D's valley end (1184.0, -867.9) and cluster C's source (1128.6, -917.8):
  - are 74.6u apart, across a spur of the same highland;

  Stock draws Alexandria's river into the valley, hides it under the spur, and lets it out again as cluster C's
  stream to the sea. The kit's `world-river` carries that lower stretch.

## Verdict

R2 closes for cluster D, for the reason 5c gave for cluster C's source: the plateau is the continental highland
(65 blocks, the tower and 83 entrance tiles), not a massif. A lawful cut must go round all of it.

Disc 1 has three falls blocks:
- the horseshoe's two, which `world-mountain --donor horseshoe` already carries in game;
- cluster D's one, which is Alexandria's.

So the horseshoe stays the only carriable falls. Adding Stream to `ENSEMBLE_PARTS`, which R2 proposed for cluster D,
is moot: the horseshoe has no stream.
